Bombay Restaurant (popularly known as Bombay Hotel) is a legendary, historic culinary landmark located on Silk Street near Kozhikode Beach. Founded in 1949 by chef Edakkad Kunhahammed, it has preserved over 75 years of Malabar heritage and remains a must-visit spot for food lovers. 
✨ Culinary Highlights
Legendary Dum Biryani: Famous for its authentic Kozhikode-style Chicken and Mutton Dum Biryani, strictly served for lunch between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM. 
Malabar Evening Snacks: Starting every afternoon, they rotate a massive daily selection of over 20 traditional savories like Unnakaya, Elanchi, and Chatti Pathiri. 
Signature Mutton Cutlet: The only local establishment famous for a unique Mutton Cutlet served with egg white. 
💡 Practical Tips for Visiting
Timing your Visit: They are open daily from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. It gets incredibly busy from 4:00 PM onwards as locals gather for evening tea and snacks before going to the beach. 
Parking Challenge: Finding a spot can be difficult due to limited dedicated space, so be prepared for tight street parking during peak hours. 
Atmosphere: It offers a casual, historic, and traditional setting rather than a contemporary fine-dining experience.
